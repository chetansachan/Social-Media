    Phone: For you / Following tabs replace the rail, avatar-composer replaces the inline text area, bottom nav is the persistent navigation, trending rail is dropped. Tablet: icon rail, right rail gone at 768 and returns at 1024.

    One thing to decide about phones

    The contract bans information that only exists on hover, and wants no clipped control. On a 360px phone, do I keep the For you / Following tabs, or drop them?

    Keep the tabs

    Two feeds, both reachable. Digests the app's main job. Costs ~48px of vertical space on a small screen.

    Drop them, infinite scroll only

    Maximum vertical room for cards. Loses the chronological vs algorithmic split the backend actually supports.

    Tell me in the terminal: "keep the tabs" or "drop the tabs" — plus anything about the breakpoints you want changed.
